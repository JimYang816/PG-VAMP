## Physical chain (§§2–10)
- Distinguish 512 grid positions, 8192 waveform FFT points and 400 unknown data
  symbols. Defaults remain 96 kHz, 21–27 kHz, 400/64/47/1 allocation, CP 2048,
  fixed unit-energy QPSK, 8 data blocks/frame and coding=none. Exact allocation,
  class/bit order, frame and LFM definitions remain in §§3–4.
- Use ortho FFT and the explicit signed-grid/FFT index mappings. Do not add
  unexplained amplitude factors, force conjugate symmetry on independent QPSK,
  or treat edge guards as strictly band-limited output (§§3–5).
- effective_fast must use the same physical path parameters as the independent
  waveform_reference. Never substitute random matrices for the physical link,
  validate H with signals generated by that same H, impose artificial ICI
  bandwidth, or normalize H columns/Frobenius norm (§6).
- Use path-dependent affine time scaling and the 8192-length finite-sum model.
  Validate CP support at every path/block/window; reject invalid frames and
  record resampling, never hide interblock interference in AWGN.
- Select data_q receiver rows and subtract known H_DP pilot contributions before
  constructing the shared 400×400 system. No top-left cropping, pilot/null
  QPSK denoising or silent rectangular-system extension (§8).
- Preserve complex-noise variance, Es/N0 and channel scaling conventions (§9).
  Never set noise from measured per-frame signal power. Main comparison uses
  ideal I/Q, ideal timing, perfect CSI and known noise; LFM timing is separate.
  A changed front end/window requires rechecking H and noise covariance.

## Algorithms (§§12–14)
- Solve systems; never call inverse/torch.linalg.inv. MMSE is full-H Cholesky
  linear MMSE with raw linear output, no disguised QPSK post-denoiser.
  VAMP uses exact SVD LMMSE and an independent Cholesky oracle; both baselines
  have zero learned parameters and are not weakened to favor PG-VAMP.
- PG-VAMP learns only raw_gaps[T] and raw_mu[T]: exactly 2T real scalars
  (16 for T=8). Preserve monotone soft gates, energy compensation, analytical
  safety majorizer, centered full-H residual, actual conditional divergence
  and sum-of-squares variance calibration (§§14.1–14.5).
- Do not symmetrize H or its mask, replace compensation with (1-m)^2,
  add compensation/safety energy to noise, use zero-centered solve_P(b),
  or substitute exact-VAMP precision/divergence identities into PG-VAMP.
- Reuse the same actual Cholesky factor/matrix for innovation, apply_B,
  divergence and variance; exact trace and Frobenius calculations are required.
- Preserve stable analytical QPSK, true divergence, explicit invalid/underflow
  branches, previous-message retention, mean-preserving precision caps and
  no-information outputs. Return final posterior, not extrinsic messages.
  Exact conditions and thresholds remain in §§14.5–14.7.
- No finite-step CG, right-hand-side stopping, cross-layer solver warm start,
  stochastic trace, silent hard-mask inference, or arbitrary forward detach.
  Only logging copies may detach. Full-network gradients follow actual
  dependencies despite the local conditional-divergence definition (§14.8).
- Jitter is not an implicit fix: if needed, it is nonnegative, explicitly
  configured/recorded and consistently included in all affected actual
  operators. Do not secretly change A and keep citing the original model.
- Do not turn PG-VAMP into standard VAMP, unfolded gradient descent or a GNN.
  Dense work can remain cubic; soft-edge counts and 16 parameters do not prove
  sparse acceleration, low memory, monotone BER or guaranteed superiority.

## Executable protection lesson from WP0

- In both independent reference message paths, branch **before** a reciprocal
  that cannot be represented, and before a precision-cap reciprocal whose
  derivative would overflow. Computing `1/vbar` and then indexing/clamping its
  result can leave `0 * Inf = NaN` in backward despite finite forward outputs.
- Preserve §14.6: zero/nonfinite/unrepresentable candidates are rejected;
  valid candidates above `1e8` retain their mean and use constant precision
  `1e8`. This is an evaluation-order repair, not a new epsilon or threshold.
- Required regressions: float64/float32 nonrepresentable reciprocal boundary,
  rejected-message backward, finite capped-message backward, unchanged candidate
  means, and complete multilayer PG parameter gradients. Keep the mathematical
  forward/gradcheck tolerances from §23 unchanged.
- Independent implementations deliberately duplicate numerical protection logic;
  apply the same source contract to each and test each, rather than sharing an
  operator that makes one oracle certify the other.

## Executable weak-channel lesson from WP5

- The trace's no-information bound and safe differentiation solve different
  problems. A finite, resolvable c must not be rejected solely to avoid backward
  overflow. Preserve the source bound and stabilize the equivalent arithmetic.
- Direct complex/real division by very small c can overflow local backward terms
  although the final derivative is representable. Real-component division alone
  still forms a dangerous x/c² term; production PG uses two sqrt(c) divisions
  for W/c and innovation/c. No detach, new cutoff or noise/channel normalization.
- Non-diagonal weak channels in a mixed weak/zero/normal batch must produce finite
  gradients without contaminating the normal sample. Test both float64 and float32;
  a diagonal weak fixture may hide the defect through zero parameter dependence.
- Diagnostic norm ratios also need stable arithmetic. Cancel a common scale in
  numerator and denominator instead of squaring extreme finite energies directly.
  This logging calculation must not feed back into the detector equations.
